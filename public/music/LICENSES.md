# Built-in music beds: licenses

All eight tracks come from **Mixkit** (mixkit.co, an Envato service). They are covered by the **Mixkit Stock Music Free License**:
https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree (the full text is in the "Stock Music Free License" modal, https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/).

## License summary (checked 2026-10-16)

- **Allowed:** commercial and non-commercial projects, free of charge. That covers podcasts, social media video posts, online marketing ads, educational use and YouTube videos. You may download, copy, modify, distribute and publicly perform the music on any web or social media platform, including internet video-on-demand services and advertisements.
- **Attribution:** not required. Mixkit welcomes it, but it is optional.
- **Not allowed:** CDs/DVDs, TV and radio broadcasts, and video games. You may not remix a track (or put it into a music-only track), claim it as your own, or register it with any rights-management or Content ID service.
- More limits are in Mixkit's User Terms (https://mixkit.co/terms/).
- If a platform raises a copyright claim, forward the details to team@mixkit.co.

**What this means for Shipreel:** using these beds under TikTok, Reels or Shorts marketing videos is allowed. Do not offer the MP3s as standalone music downloads. Do not use them in a video that goes to TV or radio broadcast.
